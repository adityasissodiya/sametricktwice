## Myth traps (use only with strong sources and careful words)

- "Hedy Lamarr invented Wi-Fi": her 1942 frequency-hopping patent is real; a direct line to
  Wi-Fi is disputed.
- "NASA invented Velcro / Teflon": both came first (Velcro in the 1950s, Teflon in 1938);
  NASA used them.
- "The microwave oven came from a melted chocolate bar": the patent is real; the candy is an
  anecdote. Say "the story goes".
- "Einstein invented the fridge": he co-patented one design (1930).
- "Edison invented the light bulb": he made a practical, long-lasting one; many came before.
